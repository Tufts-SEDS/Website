Tufts SEDS CubeSat Team

Visit the CubeSat Website

Tufts SEDS CubeSat team is on a mission to contribute to space exploration by building a CubeSat designed to detect and identify space debris. Our team, composed of students from diverse academic backgrounds, shares a common passion for unraveling the mysteries of the cosmos. Stay tuned as we work towards our goal of launching this satellite into the final frontier.
